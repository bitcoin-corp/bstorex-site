How it works

A store and a store room, in one.

Think of an app store and a file locker joined together. You put something up once. People pay to own it, or pay to download it, and the money goes to whoever made it.

Live

Buy with one tap

Open Exchange › Store in bWalletX. One sheet shows the price, you tap Allow, and it's yours, with a signed receipt in your NFTs.

Live

Put your work on chain

Mint a picture, a song or a video as an NFT from bWalletX. It lives on Bitcoin, in your wallet, and you can send it to anyone's name.

Coming

Paid downloads

Store any file and set a price to download it. Buyers pay for delivery, and you're paid in BSV straight to your wallet each time.

The idea is simple: anything digital can be sold, kept and delivered in one place, and the person who made it gets paid directly. We add each piece below only when it works.

Storage · coming

Pay for storage as long as you like.

We keep your files and recordings. You pay a little each month from your own wallet, signed in advance and time-locked so each payment can only go out when its month comes round. Stop paying, and we stop storing. Nobody holds your money up front.

Coming

Signed ahead, paid monthly

Your wallet signs one payment for each month you want, locked to its date. We can only collect a month when it arrives. Spend those coins elsewhere and the rest are cancelled.

Coming

Stop any time

If a month's payment can't be collected, you get a warning and time to top up. After that we delete the file. You're never locked in.

Coming

Recordings you can rely on

Keep a Space recording, or the audio of a spoken agreement, when everyone involved agrees. It's encrypted to the people in it, its fingerprint is sealed on chain, and whoever wants it kept pays for it. A transcript can be argued with; your own voice is much harder to dispute.
