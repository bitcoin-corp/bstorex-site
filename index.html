How it works

A store inside your wallet.

Think of an app store that lives in your wallet. A maker puts something up once. People pay to own it, and the money goes to whoever made it.

Live

Buy with one tap

Open Exchange › Store in bWalletX. One sheet shows the price, you tap Allow, and it's yours, with a signed receipt in your NFTs.

Live

Put your work on chain

Mint a picture, a song or a video as an NFT from bWalletX. It lives on Bitcoin, in your wallet, and you can send it to anyone's name.

Coming

Your own shop

Open a shop in the Store, sell your themes, art, music and files, and get 80% of each sale straight to your wallet in BSV.

The idea is simple: anything digital can be sold in one place, and the person who made it gets paid directly. We add each piece below only when it works. Keeping files and recordings now has its own home: bDriveX.
